Phase Clock

Motive

Have an indicator of the time that doesn't contribute to insomnia.

Background

I wanted to build a clock that would inform me of different bands of time without displaying the actual time.

This is because I sometimes wake up in the middle of the night. I think it's acceptable to get out of bed before one's alarm, but there is a certain hour that the only reasonable option is to try to fall back asleep. For me, right now, I have an alarm at 7am, but I think it would be unreasonable to get out of bed before 5am. Currently, I check my iPhone display for the time. I've been informed by sleep experts (people who took `PSYC 135: Dement's Sleep and Dreams`) that this interferes with my sleep and diminishes the chance I'll fall back asleep. Given how unsuccessful I am at falling back asleep when I do wake up in the middle of the night, I'm willing to try something else. However, I'm not willing to try their recommendation of just lying in bed without checking anything until my alarm goes off at 7am. This is because 7am is the outside range of when I think is acceptable to wake up. I don't want to enforce that I stay in bed until then. At one phase of my life, I strictly got out of bed at the same time every day. I don't want to do this either. In theory it is better sleep hygeine, but I found it very rigid, inflexible for shifting demands with work and life, and slows recovery if you need extra sleep one day due to preceding poor sleep. Therefore, I've landed on setting up a minimal "display" that tells me (unprompted, so only relevant if I wake up on my own) when it's acceptable to wake up. My iPhone will continue to enforce my later bound of when I must reasonable wake up on days that I feel I need an alarm.

Requirements:

  • Minimal aperature for data > only and RGB LED (interferes least with sleep)
  • Distinguishes a "can wake up" phase without alerting me

Materials:

  • RTC: PCF8523 RTC Breakout Board
  • Microcontroller: Raspberry Pi RP2040 Zero
  • RGB LED: WS2812 RGB LED Breakout modules
